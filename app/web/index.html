          Provider region
          

            
            
            Pins every request to providers headquartered and hosted in the EU/EEA/UK/CH/US/CA, with fallbacks limited to the allowlist. A model's origin and its serving location are different things: a Chinese model served from a US datacentre is allowed, and a request that cannot be served in-region fails loudly rather than silently routing elsewhere.
